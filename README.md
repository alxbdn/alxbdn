### 🛡️ What I do

I sit on both sides: I **break** systems (Active Directory audits, pentest) and I **fix** them with ANSSI-compliant hardening, turning vulnerabilities into concrete action plans.
Every measure I ship is **proved**: tests, screenshots and documentation.
I like security that is **proportionate**: the right control for the real risk, backed by ANSSI guidance, and nothing that just looks impressive.
